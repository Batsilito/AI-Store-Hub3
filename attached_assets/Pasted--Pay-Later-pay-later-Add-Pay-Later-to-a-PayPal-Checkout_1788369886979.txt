

## Pay Later [#pay-later]

Add Pay Later to a PayPal Checkout so customers can buy now and pay over time - while merchants get paid right away. Pay Later includes Buy Now Pay Later options like Pay in 4 and PayPal Credit.

[Integrate Pay Later →](/pay-later/overview/)

## Checkout essentials [#checkout-essentials]

### [Accept PayPal](/checkout/)

Add PayPal payments to a digital store so customers can pay using any funding source linked to their PayPal account.

### [Venmo](/venmo/)

Add Venmo to a digital store to accept Venmo payments for any US-based merchants and US-based consumers only.

### [Links and buttons](/payment-links-buttons/overview/)

Skip the integration and use payment links and buttons to accept PayPal payments anywhere.

### [Offer Pay Later](/pay-later/overview/)

Add Pay Later to a PayPal Checkout so a merchant can get paid right away while a customer can pay over time.

### [Apple Pay](/apple-pay/integrate/)

Add Apple Pay to accept secure, one-tap payments on customers' iOS devices or a Safari web browser.

### [Google Pay](/google-pay/integrate/)

Add Google Pay to accept fast and secure payments in a streamlined checkout experience.

### [Accept in-person payments](https://www.paypal.com/business/pos-system)

Choose from 3 point-of-sale systems to accept payments in a physical location, by connecting a terminal to PayPal's servers.

### [Accept donations](/sdk/donate/)

Add the PayPal Donate button to keep customers within a digital experience and not redirect them to a donation page elsewhere.
